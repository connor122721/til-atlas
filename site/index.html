Tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes, in public data

Which T cells actually cure cancer?

TIL therapy takes T cells out of a tumor, grows billions of them, and puts them back. Some patients are cured; most aren't. This site follows the question through public data: every TIL trial on ClinicalTrials.gov, single-cell T and B cell receptors from tumors, bulk RNA-seq from immunotherapy trials, and a model of what happens to each clone after infusion, seeded with real repertoires.
