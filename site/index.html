Tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte therapy, in data

Which T cells actually cure cancer?

TIL therapy takes T cells out of a tumor, grows billions of them, and puts them back. Some patients are cured; most aren't. This site follows the field three ways: a tracker that re-reads ClinicalTrials.gov every week, a simulator for the clones you infuse, and a lab that re-derives published single-cell results from public data.
