Regenerate this page from real data

1
Download. analysis/00_download_geo.sh GSE222750 GSE221253 on your cluster pulls GEO supplementary files (Barras et al. 2024 scTCR and bulk RNA from the Lausanne TIL trial NCT03475134). Check each paper's data-availability statement for the rest; some cohorts are controlled-access (dbGaP/EGA).
2
Score. Rscript analysis/01_score_signatures.R --seurat products.rds --meta clinical.csv subsets CD8⁺ cells, scores the gene sets in analysis/signatures/ with UCell, and writes cells.json + patients.json. Replace the starter marker panels with the published DN/DP signature genes from the paper's supplement for a faithful replication.
3
Track clones. Rscript analysis/02_clonotypes.R --airr tcr/*.tsv reads AIRR-format TCR tables, computes frequencies per sample, persistence, and Morisita-Horn overlap, and writes clones.json.
4
Publish. Copy the three JSON files into site/data/lab/ and push. The synthetic banner disappears because real exports set meta.synthetic = false.
