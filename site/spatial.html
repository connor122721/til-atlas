Spatial

Where T cells sit matters as much as how many there are. Barras et al. found CD8⁺ T cell–myeloid networks in tumors that responded to TIL therapy; checkpoint studies find the same kind of signal. This tab maps single cells in tissue and asks which cell types sit together more often than chance.

How these numbers are made

Each platform (Xenium, CosMx, imaging mass cytometry, multiplex IF) is first reduced to one table with a row per cell: sample, x and y in micrometres, a cell type, and the patient's response. analysis/spatial/export_spatial.py then, for each sample, counts every pair of cells closer than the contact radius, shuffles the cell-type labels a few hundred times to see how many contacts chance alone would give, and reports the difference as a z-score. The same script measures each CD8⁺ cell's distance to the nearest tumor cell, which separates inflamed tumors (T cells among tumor cells) from excluded ones (T cells held in the stroma).

Per-dataset recipes are in analysis/spatial/README.md; the Datasets page lists which public cohorts are wired up.
