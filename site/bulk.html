Bulk RNA-seq

Does the T and B cell content of a tumor before treatment predict who responds? Real pre-treatment RNA-seq from immune checkpoint trials, harmonized by CRI iAtlas and published in cBioPortal's datahub. One AUC per cohort: 0.5 is a coin flip; above 0.5 means responders had higher scores.

What's being measured

For each cohort, one pre-treatment biopsy per patient. Expression is log2(TPM+1), z-scored gene by gene within the cohort, and a signature score is the mean over its genes (the gene lists are in analysis/bulk/signatures.json). Rows marked “iAtlas” are scores CRI iAtlas computed and ships with the data. AUC is the probability that a randomly chosen responder scores higher than a randomly chosen non-responder; its interval comes from 1,000 bootstrap resamples within each group.

These are checkpoint-inhibitor cohorts, not TIL therapy: they ask whether the T and B cells already in a tumor predict response to releasing them. The closest TIL-therapy bulk cohort with response labels, Lauss et al. 2017 (GEO GSE100797, 25 patients), is listed on the Datasets page; analysis/bulk/geo_cohort.py scores it the same way.

Data: cBioPortal datahub (iAtlas-harmonized studies), ODbL 1.0. Derived scores on this page are shared under the same licence. Regenerate with python analysis/bulk/fetch_iatlas.py.
